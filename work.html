Dry Cleaners Platform — 5 Dashboards

End-to-end multi-role system for dry-clean businesses: Admin, Dealer, Shopkeeper, Customer, Delivery Boy. Shared catalog & pricing from Dealer, with optional shop-level add-ons and complete OTP-verified pickup & drop flow.

  • +37%Order Throughput
  • −28%Pickup Delays
  • 99.3%OTP Success
  • +22%Repeat Rate

Contents

Overview Roles & Permissions Service & Pricing Logic Booking & OTP Flow Order Lifecycle Dashboards (5) Notifications & Payments Results Learnings

Overview

Multi-tenant platform jisme Dealer ek shared catalog manage karta hai (services + base price). Sabhi Shopkeepers ko yahi catalog milta hai; wo apni shop-specific services/charges add kar sakte hain. Customer signup → service booking → auto-assignment to Delivery Boy → OTP-verified pickup & drop.

  • Multi-Tenant
  • Role-Based
  • OTP
  • Logistics

Roles & Permissions

  • Admin: global settings, tenants, compliance, analytics, payouts, disputes.
  • Dealer: master catalog (services, base price, SLAs), offers, taxes, service areas.
  • Shopkeeper: branch profile, extra services/add-ons, capacity & time-slots, QC, billing.
  • Customer: signup/login, address book, booking, tracking, payment, ratings.
  • Delivery Boy: assigned routes, OTP verify (pickup/drop), status updates, earnings.

Service & Pricing Logic

  • Dealer catalog → sabhi shopkeepers ke liye default services & base price.
  • Shop add-ons: shop-level custom services, surcharges (urgent, heavy, stain-type) & combos.
  • Price precedence: Dealer base → Shop modifier (±%) → Offer/Coupon → Tax (GST etc.).
  • Variants: garment category (Shirt, Suit, Saree…), care type (Wash, Dry Clean, Steam, Stain-care).
  • SLAs: standard vs express, holiday calendar, cut-off times.

Booking & OTP Flow

  1. Signup/Login: Customer email/phone OTP; address & preferred slot choose.
  2. Book Service: catalog se items add (qty, notes, stain images optional) → price breakdown visible.
  3. Auto-Assign: nearest available Delivery Boy (route & load balance) + shop capacity check.
  4. Pickup OTP: customer ke email/phone par OTP; Delivery Boy app par verify → Pickup Confirmed.
  5. Handover: Delivery Boy → Shopkeeper; items tagged (QR/barcode), condition photos.
  6. Delivery OTP: service complete → Delivery Boy drop → OTP verify → Delivered.

Order Lifecycle

Status pipeline: New → Assigned → Picked → In-Process → QC → Ready → Out for Delivery → Delivered (or Issue/Return).

  • QC checks: stains noted, damage flags, re-clean loop if required.
  • Tracking: live status for customer (email/SMS/push), ETA updates, proof photos.
  • Invoices: tax invoice (GST), discounts, wallet/refunds, split payments (UPI/card/cash).

Dashboards (5)

  • Admin: tenants, revenue, service levels, disputes, payouts; role/rate limits, audit logs.
  • Dealer: catalog & pricing, offers, geo-coverage, order analytics, settlement reports.
  • Shopkeeper: queue, capacity calendar, QC panel, staff shifts, inventory (chemicals/consumables).
  • Customer: orders, invoices, addresses, saved items, re-order, support tickets, ratings.
  • Delivery Boy: today’s route, OTP verify UI, map, earnings, attendance & shift adherence.

Notifications & Payments

  • Notifications: email/SMS/push at key events (assigned, pickup OTP, in-process, ready, out for delivery, delivered).
  • Payments: prepaid/postpaid, COD, wallet; refunds/adjustments; auto-invoice on delivery.
  • Security: OTP at pickup/drop, geofence on pickup/drop, tamper-proof QR tags.

Learnings

  • Dealer-led catalog keeps pricing consistent; shop add-ons give local flexibility.
  • 2-OTP checkpoints (pickup & drop) fraud/claims ko kaafi kam karte hain.
  • Capacity calendar + route balancing = timely pickups & fewer cancellations.
  • Photos (before/after) & barcode tags → faster QC & fewer disputes.
